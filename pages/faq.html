Questions Students Ask Most

Instant guidance on accounts, biometric attendance regulations, assignment evaluations, semester mark sheets, and online fee settlements.

Popular:

Use the Forgot password link on the login page and enter your verified CHARUSAT college email address (@charusat.edu.in). The security reset token is active for 30 minutes. If it expires, generate a fresh request. Students who have lost access to their university mailbox can visit the IT Help Desk in Academic Block A with their student identity card for an instant master reset.

Helpful resolution?

Accounts are populated from the central university registrar file uploaded following admission round confirmations. Ensure you enter your enrolment number without dashes or spaces (e.g., 25IT049). If newly admitted or transferred, email help@charusat.edu.in with your provisional admission slip and department details; your profile will be provisioned within 24 hours.

Helpful resolution?

Faculty upload lecture attendance daily from departmental biometric consoles. Records sync automatically to your Student Dashboard every evening at 11:00 PM. Practical lab sessions require faculty verification and may take up to 24 hours to reconcile. The percentage displayed reflects all sessions up to the preceding working day.

Helpful resolution?

Submit an attendance review petition from the Attendance Ledger on your dashboard within 7 calendar days of the lecture date. Older discrepancies require endorsement from your Head of Department. Once your course instructor reviews and signs off on the correction, the recalculated percentage reflects after the next nightly synchronization.

Helpful resolution?

In accordance with CHARUSAT academic regulations, a minimum of 75% overall attendance is mandatory to sit for end-semester university examinations. Subjects between 75% and 80% generate caution flags, and those falling below 75% trigger an automated mentor notice. Approved medical leave or institutional on-duty approvals must be filed with the Examination Cell before the semester teaching completion date.

Helpful resolution?

The interactive assignment turn-in portal accepts PDF, DOCX, ZIP, C/CPP, PY, and IPYNB files up to 25 MB per attempt. Scanned handwritten solutions should be compiled into a single clean PDF document with your Roll Number and Subject Code marked in the header. Larger dataset projects should be linked via your institutional Google Drive.

Helpful resolution?

Submission gateways lock automatically at 11:59 PM on the deadline day. Late turn-ins can only be accepted if your course instructor re-opens an individual extension window. Course policies apply a standard 10% late deduction per day past the deadline. Class-wide extensions granted by the department are reflected automatically on the assignment dashboard.

Helpful resolution?

Official results are published to your StudentHub dashboard within 4 to 6 weeks following the final theory paper. You can view subject-wise letter grades, CIE scores, SGPA, and cumulative CGPA. A digitally signed provisional mark sheet can be generated directly from the results view for internship or job verification.

Helpful resolution?

Navigate to the Campus Events Hub, select your session (e.g., Spoural, Cognizance, or Developer Conclaves), and click Reserve Seat. Your electronic entry QR badge is generated immediately and stored in your portal. Passes close 24 hours prior to event commencement or upon reaching auditorium capacity.

Helpful resolution?

Yes. You can cancel reservations from your Events dashboard up to 6 hours before the start time. This releases your seat to students on the waitlist. Students who miss two registered events without prior cancellation will lose priority registration for the subsequent term.

Helpful resolution?

CHARUSAT supports UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, Debit Cards, and RTGS/NEFT with zero surcharge. Credit card payments incur standard 1.4% gateway fees displayed prior to authorization. Official fee receipts with university tax ID and transaction reference numbers are available for instant download in PDF format.

Helpful resolution?

Bank clearing webhooks may occasionally experience temporary reconciliation delays. Payments typically update to Paid within 2 to 4 hours. If your status has not reconciled after 24 hours, forward your bank transaction reference (UTR) to accounts@charusat.edu.in. Do not make a duplicate payment while reconciliation is in progress.
