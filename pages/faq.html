Questions students ask most

Use the Forgot password link on the login page and enter your college email address. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes; if it expires, request a fresh one rather than reusing the old mail. Students who have lost access to their college mailbox must apply in person at the IT Help Desk in Academic Block A with their identity card.

Accounts are created from the university enrolment file, which is uploaded roughly a week after admission formalities close. Check that you are entering the roll number exactly as printed on your fee receipt, for example 21CE045, with no spaces. If it is still rejected, write to help@studenthub.edu with your enrolment number and department and the record is normally added within two working days.

Faculty mark attendance from the classroom terminal and the records sync to your dashboard every night at 11 PM. Laboratory and tutorial sessions are posted by the lab in-charge and occasionally take an extra day. The percentage you see always reflects classes conducted up to the previous working day.

Raise a correction request from the Attendance tab within seven days of the class date. Anything older needs a written approval from your Head of Department before it can be considered. The subject teacher either approves or rejects the request with a remark, and approved corrections appear after the next nightly sync rather than immediately.

The dashboard flags any subject below 80% in amber and below 75% in red, and a copy of the warning goes to your faculty mentor. Under university regulations a student below 75% in a subject is not permitted to sit that subject's end-semester examination unless the shortfall is condoned on medical grounds. Condonation applications, with supporting documents, must reach the Examination Cell before the last teaching day of the semester.

The submission box accepts PDF, DOCX, ZIP and IPYNB files up to 25 MB per attempt. Scanned handwritten work should be combined into one PDF instead of being uploaded as loose images. If your file is larger than the limit, upload it to your departmental drive and paste the link into the remarks box.

The upload box closes at the deadline and can only be reopened by the subject teacher. Reopened submissions are tagged as late in the grading sheet and most departments apply a 10% deduction. Extensions granted for the whole class are announced on the notice board and applied automatically, so there is no need to write in separately.

Results are published within an hour of being declared by the Examination Cell, usually four to six weeks after the last paper. The Results tab shows subject-wise grades, SGPA and cumulative CGPA, and a provisional mark sheet can be downloaded from the same screen. Printed transcripts are issued by the Examination Cell against a written application and the prescribed fee.

Open the Events page, pick the session and use the Register button; the seat is confirmed straight away and your pass appears under My Events. Registration closes 24 hours before the start time or as soon as hall capacity is reached, whichever comes first. Carry your student identity card, because passes are verified at the door.

Cancel from My Events at least six hours before the session so the seat can be released to the waiting list. Students who miss two registered events in a semester without cancelling lose priority booking for the rest of the term. Paid workshops follow the refund rule printed on the individual event page.

Net banking, UPI, debit cards and NEFT are supported through the university payment gateway. Credit cards attract a gateway charge of 1.8%, which is shown on screen before you confirm. Demand drafts are still accepted at Counter 3 of the Accounts Office but must be deposited at least three working days before the deadline.

Gateway confirmations sometimes arrive late and the status usually settles on its own within 24 hours. If it is still pending after that, send the transaction reference number and the date of the debit to accounts@studenthub.edu. Do not pay a second time — duplicate transactions take up to three weeks to refund and the late-payment charge is not waived in the meantime.
