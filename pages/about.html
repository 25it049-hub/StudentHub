The office behind the portal

StudentHub is the student services portal of Sabarmati Institute of Technology, Ahmedabad. It went live on 15 July 2024 and now carries attendance, results, fee receipts, event registration and grievances for 12,400 students across 38 departments and centres.

Why we built it

One portal, because four of them was three too many

Until 2024 attendance sat in a desktop application, results in a PDF on the notice board, fees at Counter 3 and everything else in a WhatsApp group. This is what replaced that.

Our mission

Cut the time a student spends walking between offices down to nothing. Every record, form, notice and payment sits behind one login, and every request raised through the portal gets a written reply — with a ticket number — inside two working days.

Our vision

By 2028, every departmental circular, examination form and grievance should begin and close inside StudentHub, with paper kept only where a GTU or AICTE statute still demands a wet signature. Nobody should miss a deadline for reading the wrong board.

Why StudentHub

Five things it does that the old system did not

None of this is clever. It is mostly a matter of putting the same information in one place and keeping it current.

  • One login, eight offices Accounts, Examination, Library, Hostel, Transport, Training & Placement, your department office and the student council all publish to the same feed.
  • Attendance that warns you early Subject-wise percentages refresh by 8 PM the same day and flag you at 78%, not at 74% when the detention list has already gone up.
  • Records that outlive the semester Marksheets, fee receipts, bonafide letters and workshop certificates stay downloadable for six years after you graduate.
  • Built here, fixed here Two full-time developers and four interns from the IT department maintain it. A bug report joins the same queue as a fee query and is answered the same way.
  • Light enough for hostel Wi-Fi Every page stays under 400 KB, works without JavaScript for reading, and does not need an app install to check a result at midnight.

Milestones

Twenty-eight years, in short

The dates the institute marks in its calendar. Everything else is in the annual report.

  1. 1998

    Three branches, one rented block

    Founded on Drive-In Road with 180 sanctioned seats in Civil, Mechanical and Electrical Engineering. Eleven faculty members, no library of its own.

  2. 2003

    Move to the University Road campus

    Forty-two acres, four academic blocks and the first hostel. Computer Engineering and Information Technology admitted their first intake the same July.

  3. 2008

    Affiliation transferred to GTU

    Along with every other engineering college in the state after Gujarat Technological University was constituted. Semester pattern replaced the annual examination.

  4. 2016

    Central Placement Cell set up

    Recruitment moved from individual departments to one office in Block A. The 2016 season closed with 214 offers from 31 companies.

  5. 2024

    StudentHub goes live

    Attendance, results, fees, event booking and grievances moved onto one login on 15 July, retiring four separate systems and the paper leave register.

  6. 2026

    NAAC A+ in the third cycle

    Re-accredited in January with a CGPA of 3.42, and the Innovation Lab in Block D opened to all departments rather than final-year projects alone.
