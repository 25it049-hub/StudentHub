Our Purpose

Our Purpose & Mission

Bridging academic administration with cutting-edge engineering so every student can focus on building, learning, and innovating.

Our Mission

Eliminate campus administrative friction entirely. Every mark sheet, fee receipt, timetable notice, and grievance operates behind one intuitive interface. Requests submitted through the portal receive a verified, trackable ticket with guaranteed resolution updates inside two working days.

Our Vision

Foster a paperless, instantaneous digital ecosystem across the entire 120-acre CHARUSAT campus. By integrating cloud intelligence, real-time sync, and modern UI/UX, we ensure no student ever misses a scholarship, examination deadline, or campus opportunity due to outdated noticeboards.

Historic Milestones

Twenty-Eight Years of Excellence

From an ambitious educational foundation in 1998 to a premier global university campus.

1998

Three Foundational Branches

Founded on Drive-In Road with 180 sanctioned seats in Civil, Mechanical and Electrical Engineering under visionary educational trusts.

2003

Move to Changa University Road Campus

Inaugurated the 120-acre lush green campus with modern academic blocks, research labs, hostels, and first intakes for Computer & IT Engineering.

2008

Transition to Autonomous University System

Established autonomous academic curriculum with rigorous semester evaluations, international collaborative research, and industrial electives.

2016

Central Placement & Innovation Cell

Launched the unified Training & Placement headquarters in Academic Block A, expanding to 100+ national and multinational hiring partners.

2024

Launch of Unified StudentHub Portal

Retired fragmented legacy paper registers; combined attendance, examination results, fee receipts, and digital ticketing into one secure web system.

2026

NAAC A+ Accreditation & 3D Portal Upgrade
