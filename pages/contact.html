Student services

Contact the help desk

One desk for portal problems, fee queries and examination paperwork. Written queries are answered within two working days; anything marked urgent is picked up the same day.

Raise a query

Fields marked with an asterisk are required. Quote your roll number in the message so the desk can open your record while replying.
