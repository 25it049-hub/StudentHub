Registration Guidelines & Code of Conduct

Registration closes 24 hours prior

No on-spot walk-ins are permitted for technical workshops and lab setups due to lab hardware configuration schedules.

Single seat per authenticated account

Proxy seat reservations on behalf of others are automatically scrubbed, resetting priority rank on subsequent waitlists.

Waitlist releases clear sequentially. When notified via SMS & portal, you have 2 hours to confirm before the pass cascades down.
