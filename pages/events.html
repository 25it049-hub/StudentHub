Before you register

  • Registration closes 24 hours before start time There are no spot entries for lab and workshop sessions — the department has to raise a consumables indent the day before.
  • One login, one seat Booking on a friend's behalf is cancelled by the coordinator without notice, and both enrolment numbers lose priority on the next waitlist.
  • Waitlists clear in order on the morning of the event You get a portal notification and an SMS on the number in your profile. Confirm within two hours or the seat moves down the list.
  • Certificates take five working days Workshop and drive certificates appear under Profile, in Documents. Nothing is issued at the venue.
