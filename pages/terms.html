Student Charter, IT Acceptable Use Policy & Code of Academic Integrity for Charotar University of Science and Technology.

Effective Date: Academic Year 2026–2027 · Changa Campus, Gujarat
🏛️

1. Institutional Enrolment & Identity Verification

By registering on the CHARUSAT StudentHub portal, you certify that all information submitted—including your Full Name, Roll / Enrolment Number, University Email (@charusat.edu.in), Mobile Number, Programme, and Academic Year—is true, complete, and matches your official admission records at Charotar University of Science and Technology. Providing false or misleading credentials is a punishable offence under University Ordinance Section 14.

🔐

2. Credential Security & Individual Responsibility

Each student is solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their portal password and digital access tokens.

  • You must never share your StudentHub login credentials with peers, external entities, or third-party automated tools.
  • Any actions performed under your authenticated account—including exam registrations, course elections, lab submissions, and inquiry filings—are legally attributed to your student identity.
  • If you suspect your credentials have been compromised, immediately reset your password and notify the University Registrar at help@charusat.edu.in.
⏱️

3. Mandatory Attendance Policy (75% Threshold)

In accordance with CHARUSAT Academic Regulations, every enrolled student is required to maintain a minimum of 75% cumulative biometric attendance across all theory lectures, lab practicals, and tutorial sessions. Students falling below this threshold will be flagged as Low Attendance and may be debarred from mid-semester and university end-semester examinations without prior notice.

💻

4. IT Infrastructure & Acceptable Use Policy

The StudentHub platform and university computing facilities must be used exclusively for legitimate educational, research, and campus administrative purposes. Prohibited activities include:

  • Attempting unauthorized access to peer accounts, admin endpoints, or backend API controllers.
  • Automated scraping, denial-of-service attempts, or injection attacks on portal servers.
  • Uploading malicious binaries, defamatory content, or copyright-infringing materials through assignment or event file inputs.
💳

5. Fee Structure & Digital Receipts

Term tuition fees, exam fees, and lab dues must be paid within university-mandated deadlines. Digital receipts generated through StudentHub carry a cryptographically validated confirmation code and serve as official university payment vouchers for hostel, transportation, and examination clearances.

🛡️

6. Data Privacy & University Audit Trail

All student activity, authentication events, IP addresses, and administrative actions are logged in the University Audit Ledger for security compliance. CHARUSAT does not sell or share student data with commercial third parties. Student data is safeguarded under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) and university governance protocols.
